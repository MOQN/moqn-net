![Main Image](./images/_main.jpg)

A new-media dance performance presented in the 2019 China Shanghai International Arts Festival’s Rising Artists’ Works program. The work draws on Shan Hai Jing and uses motion-tracked figures, live visuals, sound, and projection mapping.

The performance combines choreography with digital figures that respond to the dancers’ movements. Media-art work included creature design, software and interface development, live visual control, and stage-layout planning.

## Concept

Digital creatures were designed with different proportions and movement behavior, such as extended arms, short legs, and bird-like forms. The dancers rehearsed responses to these figures.

Live-drawn environmental visuals and real-time media control allowed the performers and media artists to adjust material during the performance.

## Development

Algorithmic visuals were developed in openFrameworks with a custom control interface. Later sequences used 3D models rigged in Unity and controlled through OSC from an iPad.

Resolume Arena was used for projection mapping and media cues. Stage layouts and sensing areas were planned through 3D mock-ups. The system combined motion capture, semi-transparent screens, real-time graphics, and sound.
